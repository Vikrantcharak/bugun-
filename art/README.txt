DRAWINGS
========

These drawings stand in where we don't have a photograph yet. They move
a little: the movement is written inside each file, so there is nothing
else to load.

  liocichla.svg      The Bugun bird: blinks, tilts its head, flicks its tail
  rhododendron.svg   The rhododendron walk: petals fall, mist drifts
  sunrise.svg        Sunrise and sunset: the sun climbs, birds cross
  fruit.svg          Fruit, food and juice: leaves stir, light passes over

When a real photo exists (taken in Bichom, with every person's yes, and
for the bird: taken without recorded calls), put it in the photos folder
and change the drawing's name to the photo's name in index.html.

The small ink sketches at the top of each part are in assets/site.js
(look for SKETCH).
