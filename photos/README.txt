PHOTOS FOR THE WEBSITE
======================

Easiest: upload your photos with ANY names and tell Claude. They will be
sorted, renamed, made smaller and captioned for you.

To replace a photo yourself, upload the new one with exactly the same
name. Keep it near 1600 px wide and 200 KB (https://squoosh.app is free).
Shoot on a phone, held sideways, in early or late light.

Rules: taken in Bichom. No stock photos, no AI pictures of people or
birds. A yes from every person in the photo; a child needs a parent's yes.
No photo that shows where the nettle grows or where a bird nests.

DISHES
------
For Bamboo and food: one photo per dish, named dish-1.jpg, dish-2.jpg …
then write the name in settings.js next to that dish (photo: "dish-1.jpg").

CAPTIONS
--------
Each photo has one line under it, in the page file. Bugun name first where
the community has one, then English. Name the person only with their yes.
