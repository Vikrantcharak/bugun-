PHOTOS FOR THE WEBSITE
======================

Easiest: upload your photos with ANY names and tell Claude. They will be
sorted, renamed and made smaller for you.

If you want to do it yourself, use exactly these names. Each one replaces
the drawing in that spot automatically:

  hero.jpg       The big picture at the very top. Wide (landscape). Your best view.
  homestay.jpg   The house from outside. Upright (portrait).
  room.jpg       A room: bed and window.
  host.jpg       You / your family.

  bridge.jpg     The old wooden bridge. Most important after hero.jpg.
  river.jpg      The river.
  fishing.jpg    Fishing: casting the net, or with a rod.
  lichini.jpg    On the path up to Lichini, or the view from the top.
  bamboo.jpg     Bamboo baskets, weaving, bamboo forest.
  food.jpg       A plate or the meal being cooked over the fire.
  sunrise.jpg    Sunrise.
  sunset.jpg     Sunset.
  birds.jpg      Birding in the forest (people with binoculars, or a bird).
  liocichla.jpg  A real photo of the Bugun Liocichla, only if you took it
                 or have permission. Replaces the drawing.

  gallery-1.jpg, gallery-2.jpg, gallery-3.jpg ...
                 Any number of extra photos. They appear in a "Photos"
                 section in order. Don't skip a number.

TIPS
  • Names lowercase, exactly as above. .jpg .jpeg .png .webp all work.
  • Keep each photo under about 500 KB (https://squoosh.app makes them smaller).
  • Visitors can tap any photo to see it full size.
