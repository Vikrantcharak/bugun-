Birds · The Bugun bird

A small bird with a long name.

Bugun liocichla, Liocichla bugunorum. A drawing, until we have a photograph taken without recorded calls.

The Bugun liocichla was first seen by a visiting birdwatcher in 1995 and named by scientists in 2006. It is critically endangered. Very few exist, and nearly all of them live in one small patch of high forest, on land our people hold.

The patrol team’s latest count is [the latest count from the patrol team].

The bird does not live at Bichom. Its forest lies between Singchung and the Eaglenest boundary, further from Bichom than Singchung, which is itself about 14 km away in a straight line.

We decided to keep it. In January 2017 the forest between Singchung and the Eaglenest boundary became the Singchung Bugun Village Community Reserve, 17 square kilometres. Young Bugun patrollers walk it.

You will meet the bird only with a Bugun guide. We do not play recorded calls to pull it out, and we do not tell anyone where it nests. If it does not show, the forest is still worth the walk.

    Made here · Nettle cloth

    Cloth from the nettle.

    Shaung: a stem, held in the hand.

    [The Bugun name for the nettle.]the nettle

    [In the makers’ own words: who made nettle cloth, who remembers how, and who is making it again.]— [who said it]

    Here are the steps, as the people who do this work tell them.

    1. Cut and peel

      June to August, green stems only. Peeled and boiled the same day, or the bark goes hard.

    2. Boil with oak ash

      An iron pan, sieved oak ash, seven to eight hours over a steady fire.

    3. Beat, rinse, dry

      Struck on a rock at the river and rinsed until it washes white, then hung in the sun.

    4. Clay

      A soft pale clay, dug and sieved, is worked in. Five or six days drying, then it is beaten out.

    5. Card

      Drawn between two toothed cards until the fibre opens.

    6. Spin

      On the Chhauk, a bamboo spindle with a hard seed for a whorl.

    We do not say where the nettle grows, and it is not for visitors to pick.
